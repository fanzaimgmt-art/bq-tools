# Obra GTM + Sales Playbook
**Product:** Obra — AI suite for US construction & home-service contractors  
**Flagship:** Video Brain (walkthrough video → Spanish translation + step-by-step plan + estimate + upsell suggestions)  
**Founder:** Moshe (BQ), solo, cash-tight, cold-email stack ready  
**Goal:** First paying customer in 30 days, ladder to $5K/mo tier  

---

## 1. ICP — Beachhead Segments (Ranked)

### Rank 1: ADU / Residential Remodeling Contractors (LA + Southern California)
**Why this first:**
- Job values: $80K–$250K per ADU project, $30K–$100K kitchen/bath remodels
- Almost universally use Mexican/Spanish-speaking crews on-site
- Estimating is their #1 bottleneck — most use Excel or yellow legal pads
- Payment lag kills cashflow — "Get Paid Faster" solves a daily pain
- Moshe already has this database via Apify scrape + GMass campaign running
- LA is oversaturated with this segment — competition is sloppy, no one digitizes
- Decision-maker is the owner/GC — one conversation to close
- Their crews ALREADY can't understand complex English instructions — Video Brain solves an immediate on-site safety + productivity problem

**Firmographics:** 3–25 employees, $1M–$10M annual revenue, licensed GC or specialty contractor, LA/OC/SB/SD metro  
**Pain signals:** "We lose money on change orders," "My foreman doesn't always understand what I need," "I hate waiting 30–60 days to get paid"

### Rank 2: Pool & Spa Contractors (LA, Phoenix, Las Vegas)
**Why second:**
- $40K–$150K per job, high upsell opportunity (heaters, automation, waterfalls)
- Spanish-crew problem identical to remodeling
- Estimating is notoriously complex (gunite, plumbing, electrical)
- Seasonality means they need to close fast in spring — urgency built in
- Smaller network than remodeling, but tighter referral loops

### Rank 3: Roofing Contractors (Hail-market cities: DFW, Denver, Phoenix, Houston)
**Why third:**
- High volume, $8K–$25K per job, insurance claims = tons of paperwork
- Spanish crews = universal
- Pain: insurance supplements, re-inspection docs, crew instructions
- Higher digital adoption than remodeling (many already use AccuRoofing/EagleView)
- Harder to get on-site visits — mostly phone/Zoom sales

**Decision: Attack Rank 1 (LA ADU/remodeling) first. Moshe has the list, the geography, and the existing cold email infrastructure.**

## 3. The Sales Pitch

### 30-Second Hook (Phone/Cold Call/DM)
> "Hey [Name], quick question — when your crew starts a job, how do you make sure everyone knows exactly what to do, especially if they don't all speak English? We built an AI for contractors like you: you take a 5-minute walkthrough video of the job site, and it spits out a full work plan, a Spanish crew sheet, and a price estimate in minutes. First project's free. You want me to show you what it looks like on one of your own jobs?"

**Key elements:**
1. Open with their pain (crew communication), not the product
2. One concrete deliverable per sentence
3. "Your own jobs" — not a demo, personalized
4. Close is frictionless: "free first project"

---

### Demo Flow (Video Brain Personalized Demo)

**Step 1 — Before the call:** Ask them to send a 3–5 minute walkthrough video of an active job site. Can be iPhone footage. Tell them it's confidential and for demo purposes only.

**Step 2 — Run it live:** Run their video through Obra Video Brain on screen share. Talk through what the AI is catching as it processes.

**Step 3 — Reveal:** Show the Spanish crew sheet first (emotional hit for Spanish-crew owners), then the work plan, then the price estimate, then the upsell suggestions.

**Step 4 — The question:** "Is this close to what you'd actually write up?" (They say yes, or they correct you — both are buying signals.)

**Step 5 — ROI bridge:** "How long does it take you to write something like this manually?" (Answer: 1–3 hours.) "This took 4 minutes. How many projects do you run a month?"

**Step 6 — Close:** "I can set you up with 5 free analyses this week so you can try it on real jobs. After that it's $297/month. Want to start today?"

---

### Objection Handling

**"It's too expensive."**
> "What does one miscommunication with your crew cost you — materials re-ordered, rework, a day of lost labor? Usually $1,500–$5,000. One prevented mistake a month more than pays for this. And the first 5 projects are free."

**"I already use [Jobber / CompanyCam / Buildertrend]."**
> "Those are great for tracking work. This is different — it's what happens before the job starts. It's the plan, the translation for your crew, and the estimate that feeds into your Jobber job. These work together, not instead of each other."

**"My guys won't use an app."**
> "Your guys don't use anything. YOU use it. You run the video, it generates a crew sheet in Spanish, you print it or send it to WhatsApp. Your foreman doesn't need an account or a login. It's just a PDF he reads."

**"I need to think about it."**
> "Completely fair. Let me set you up with the free trial right now so you have something real to think about. Send me a video from your next job and you'll have the plan in your inbox before end of day."

## 4. Channels — Ranked by Speed to Revenue (Solo Founder)

### Priority Stack (Weekly Action Cadence)

**Channel 1: Cold Email (Start Immediately)**
- Already has Apify scrape of LA remodeling contractors + GMass stack
- Target: 100 emails/day, 5 days/week = 500/week
- Subject line: "Free AI plan from your job site video"
- Body: 4 sentences max. Lead with the Spanish crew pain. One CTA: "Send me a 3-min walkthrough video, I'll send you the AI work plan free."
- Response rate target: 2–4% = 10–20 replies/week
- Follow-up sequence: Day 3 (different angle — cost of estimating manually), Day 7 (case study or result from a real project), Day 14 (break-up email with free offer)

**Channel 2: DMs on Instagram + Facebook (20 per day)**
- Search hashtags: #LAcontractor #ADUbuilder #remodeling #garageconversion #LArealtor
- Message: same hook as phone, but shorter. "Hey — do your guys speak Spanish? We built something that might save you 2 hours per job."
- Target contractor FB groups: California Contractors Licensing Board Group, LA General Contractors Network, ADU Builders SoCal
- Post value: "Here's what an AI crew plan looks like for a $180K ADU job" (screenshot, blur client info)

**Channel 3: Job-Site Visits (Wednesdays)**
- Pick one day/week for in-person visits to active job sites in LA
- Approach the GC or project manager on-site
- Show on phone: "Got 2 minutes? I built something for contractors. Can I show you on your phone?"
- Leave a card with QR code to free first project
- Target: 5 job sites per Wednesday = 20 GC contacts/month
- Highest conversion rate of any channel (people buy from people they meet)

**Channel 4: Referrals from First Customers**
- Week 3 of every client relationship: "Who else do you know who has the same crew/estimating headaches?"
- Offer: 1 free month for every referral that signs up
- Contractor networks are tight — one happy customer in a niche can refer 5–10 peers

**Channel 5: Contractor FB Groups**
- Join 5–10 LA-area and national contractor Facebook groups
- Post 2x/week: educational content (not ads): "How to prep Spanish crew instructions in 5 minutes," "3 things GCs get wrong on ADU estimates"
- No pitching in posts — let curiosity drive DMs
- Respond to every "I need help with estimates" or "crew communication" post within 1 hour

**Channel 6: Local Trade Events (Months 2–3)**
- LA Building Industry Association, NARI LA Chapter, ADU Summit events
- Sponsor a table if budget allows ($200–$500). Otherwise attend and work the room.
- Bring printed before/after: manual estimate page vs Obra output for same job
- Lower priority until first 3 clients are locked

**Weekly Action Budget (Solo Founder):**
- Mon–Fri: 100 cold emails/day via GMass (1 hour setup/day)
- Mon, Wed, Fri: 20 DMs/day (30 min)
- Wednesday: Job site visits (4 hours)
- Tuesday, Thursday: Follow-up replies + demo calls (2–3 hours/day)
- Weekend: Content for FB groups (1 post, 1 hour)

## 5. Onboarding Flow — First "Wow" Fast

**Goal: Contractor goes from signup to first wow in under 10 minutes**

**Step 1 — Instant access (no friction):** Sign up with email only. No credit card. Immediate access to upload page.

**Step 2 — Prompt the video:** "Upload a 3–5 minute walkthrough of any active job. No script needed. Just walk and talk."

**Step 3 — Processing screen (3–5 minutes):** Show a live progress feed: "Identifying work items... Translating to Spanish... Building estimate... Generating upsells..."

**Step 4 — Results reveal:** Show output in three tabs:
1. Spanish Crew Sheet (WhatsApp-ready PDF)
2. Work Plan (step-by-step, numbered)
3. Price Estimate (line items, editable)

**Step 5 — One-click actions:**
- "Send to WhatsApp" (opens WhatsApp with pre-filled message + PDF)
- "Download PDF"
- "Edit prices" (unlocks price list setup)

**Step 6 — Moshe's personal follow-up (within 2 hours):** Text or call the contractor. "Hey, it's Moshe from Obra. Did you get the plan for your job? Does it match what you'd normally write up?" — This human touch is the difference between a free user and a paying customer.

**Step 7 — Convert to paid:** After 3–5 free projects (or after they run out of free credits), offer Pro at $997/month with "lock in your price list and we'll save it for every future job."

**Time to first wow:** Under 10 minutes from signup  
**Time to first paid invoice:** Target 7 days

**Goals:** 1 paying customer

**Week 2 Milestone:** 1 paying customer (any tier), 2 more in pipeline

**Week 4 Milestone:** $1,500+ MRR, 3 case study quotes, 10+ in pipeline

## 7. Lead Magnets + Content

### Lead Magnet 1: "Free AI Estimate from Your Job Video"
- Landing page: one field (upload video), one button ("Get My AI Plan")
- No email required until results are generated (removes friction)
- After results: "Save and share this plan — sign up free"
- This IS the product. The lead magnet and the product demo are the same thing.
- Promote via: cold email CTA, FB group posts, job-site cards

### Lead Magnet 2: "Spanish Crew Sheet Template (Editable PDF)"
- For contractors who aren't ready to try the video tool
- 1-page PDF: task names in English + Spanish, common instructions, measurements
- Gate with email: "Download the free Spanish crew sheet template"
- Email follow-up sequence: Day 3 → "Here's the AI version of this same sheet from a real video"

### Lead Magnet 3: "The 5 Most Expensive Crew Mistakes on ADU Projects (And How AI Fixes Them)"
- Blog post / PDF / Facebook post
- Each mistake → how Obra prevents it
- Distributed in FB groups, via cold email, linked from profile bio
- Not a download gate — pure value. Converts through trust.

### Lead Magnet 4: "What Does a $180K ADU Actually Cost? AI Breakdown"
- Show a real (anonymized) project estimate generated by Video Brain
- Line items, Spanish crew sheet excerpt, upsell suggestions
- This educates homeowners AND contractors
- Contractors share it with their clients → Obra gets brand exposure

### Content Cadence (Solo Founder — Sustainable):
- 2 FB group posts/week (value, no pitch): 1 hour
- 1 LinkedIn post/week (targeting GCs, PMs): 30 min
- Monthly: 1 case study with a paying customer (screenshot + quote + before/after time)
