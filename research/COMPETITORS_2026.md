# Obra Competitive Intelligence — Battle Plan 2026

**Researched:** June 21, 2026  
**Product:** Obra — AI software suite for construction & home-service contractors  
**Positioning:** "Get Paid Faster" | Flagship: Video Brain (video walkthrough → Spanish crew translation + work plan + estimate + upsells)  
**Target:** LA remodeling contractors. WTP up to $5,000/month at high tiers.

---

## 1. Competitor Profiles

### 1.1 Togal.ai
- **What it does:** AI-powered construction takeoff from 2D plan drawings. Computer vision reads blueprints, auto-generates material quantities with claimed 98% accuracy. Cuts takeoff time 80%. No field management, no invoicing, no crew tools.
- **Price:** $299/user/month (billed annually) — Growth tier. Business = custom quote (4+ users).
- **Who it's for:** Commercial estimators, large GCs, institutional builders (hospitals, high-rises). NOT for residential remodelers.
- **Weakness for Obra:** Takeoff-only — stops at quantities, outputs to a spreadsheet. No path from scan to client proposal. No Spanish. No video input. No crew instructions. No payment flow. Completely irrelevant to a tile contractor who walks a kitchen before work begins.

---

### 1.2 Handoff.ai
- **What it does:** "AI Teammate" for residential remodelers and handymen. Voice-to-estimate (talk scope → AI builds estimate), photo/file uploads, AI proposals, QuickBooks sync, client portal, milestone billing. Closest feature-adjacent competitor to Obra. Has "AI site walkthroughs" on Pro/Custom plans.
- **Price:**
  - Flex: $149/mo (or $119/mo annual) — 50 AI credits, 2 users, 3 PDF uploads/mo
  - Pro: $299/mo ($239/mo annual) — unlimited AI, 5 users, full features, site walkthrough
  - Custom: $599/mo ($479/mo annual) — larger crews, advanced PM features (many "coming soon")
- **Who it's for:** Residential remodelers and handymen, solo to small crew. US-only pricing/labor data updated daily. 10,000+ contractors.
- **Weakness for Obra:**
  - No Spanish crew translation — bilingual support is in the base product description but crew-facing crew-instructions-in-Spanish is not a feature
  - "AI site walkthroughs" = estimate from photos/notes, NOT a video-to-work-plan system
  - No video brain — no video upload → identifies work scope automatically
  - Custom plan advanced features (scheduling, daily logs, cost codes) still "coming soon" as of 2026
  - No trilingual UI
  - Payment processing fees 2.85–3.35% per transaction (margin leak)
  - No upsell engine — doesn't suggest add-on work from the video analysis

---

### 1.3 OnSite (contractorai.work)
- **What it does:** AI estimating, invoicing, CRM for contractors. 60-second estimates via "20+ trade calculators." Website, auto-text-back, review automation, lead management on Premium.
- **Price:**
  - Base: $34.99/mo — unlimited estimates, invoicing, CRM, mobile app
  - Premium: $299/mo — adds FREE website, auto text-back, review automation, lead management
- **Who it's for:** Budget-conscious contractors wanting simple estimating. iOS/Android/Web.
- **Weakness for Obra:** No AI video analysis. No crew translation. No job-site walkthrough input. Trade calculators are pre-built formulas, not AI scene understanding. Extremely low-end ($34.99) suggests thin product.

---

### 1.4 Contractor Foreman
- **What it does:** All-in-one construction PM — estimates, invoices, scheduling (Gantt/CPM), time cards with GPS, QuickBooks integration, safety logs, client portal, change orders, RFIs, purchase orders, bid manager. Most transparent pricing in the category.
- **Price (annual billing):**
  - Basic: $49/mo (1 user)
  - Standard: $105/mo (3 users)
  - Plus: $166/mo (8 users) — adds scheduling, QuickBooks, time cards
  - Pro: $221/mo (15 users) — adds client portal, takeoffs
  - Unlimited: $332/mo (unlimited users)
- **Who it's for:** Small-to-mid GCs and remodelers wanting a full PM suite at affordable price. Praised by Forbes as easiest to use.
- **Weakness for Obra:** No AI anywhere in the product. No video input. No Spanish/multilingual crew tools. No automatic work plan from job-site conditions. Traditional PM software with manual data entry — the exact pain point Obra eliminates. Scheduling/QuickBooks locked behind Plus tier ($166+).

---

### 1.5 Buildertrend
- **What it does:** Full construction PM platform — scheduling, bidding, client communication, daily logs, time tracking, budgeting, QuickBooks/Xero integration. Unlimited users on all plans.
- **Price:** Moved to volume-based custom quotes in 2026 (removed published prices). Historical tiers: Essential ~$399-499/mo, Advanced ~$699-799/mo, Complete ~$999-1,099/mo. Annual discount ~10%. Most mid-market contractors: $8K-$10K/year.
- **Who it's for:** Residential builders and remodelers, $500K–$30M+ in annual construction volume. Strong in home building.
- **Weakness for Obra:** Zero AI. No video analysis. No Spanish crew translation. Priced for established companies with office staff — overwhelming for solo or small crews. Opaque pricing (requires sales call). No "get paid faster" angle; billing is complex and back-office-heavy.

---

### 1.6 Jobber
- **What it does:** Field service management — scheduling, quoting, invoicing, client reminders, GPS tracking, QuickBooks sync, online booking, review automation. Newest tier adds AI Receptionist (automated phone booking) and Marketing Suite.
- **Price (monthly, no commitment):**
  - Core (Individual): $39/mo (1 user)
  - Connect (Individual): $119/mo; Connect (Team, 5 users): $169/mo
  - Grow (Individual): $199/mo; Grow (Team, 10 users): $349/mo
  - Plus (15 users): $599/mo — includes AI Receptionist + Marketing Suite
  - Annual saves ~35%
- **Who it's for:** Home service businesses — HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, cleaning, electrical. 200K+ pros. General field service, not construction-specific.
- **Weakness for Obra:** No video analysis. No crew translation. No AI estimate from site conditions. Not built for remodeling projects with complex multi-phase scopes. AI Receptionist is scheduling-bot only, not estimating AI. No Spanish crew instructions. Estimate creation is still manual.

---

### 1.7 Housecall Pro
- **What it does:** Home service platform — scheduling, dispatching, invoicing, review automation, GPS tracking, marketing tools, QuickBooks sync, recurring service plans.
- **Price:**
  - Basic: $59/mo annual ($79/mo monthly) — 1 user, no GPS, no QuickBooks
  - Essentials: $149/mo annual ($189/mo monthly) — up to 5 users, GPS, QuickBooks
  - MAX: $299/mo annual ($329/mo monthly) — up to 8 users; extra users $35/mo each
  - Realistic all-in cost: 2–3x headline price when add-ons, payment processing (2.59–3.49%), and extra users included
- **Who it's for:** 200K+ home service businesses. HVAC, plumbing, cleaning, landscaping, electrical. Not construction/remodeling.
- **Weakness for Obra:** No AI estimating. No video analysis. No Spanish crew tools. Not remodeling-focused — designed for simple service calls (arrive, fix, invoice), not multi-week projects. Add-on trap well-documented (users pay 2–3x headline price). No scope generation.

---

### 1.8 ServiceTitan
- **What it does:** Enterprise field service platform — dispatching, price book, flat-rate selling, marketing automation, call tracking, financing integrations, advanced reporting. Per-technician pricing. Implementation takes months.
- **Price:** Not published. User-reported: $125–$398/technician/month. For 5 techs: $625–$1,990/mo. Annual contracts required. Implementation fee $1,500–$3,000+. First-year total cost for small shop: $7,500–$24,000.
- **Who it's for:** Established HVAC, plumbing, electrical companies. Minimum viable use: 10+ technicians, $3M+ revenue. Enterprise-grade.
- **Weakness for Obra:** Completely wrong for remodeling contractors — built for dispatch-based service calls, not project-based remodeling. No Spanish. No video. No AI estimating. Prohibitively expensive and complex for Obra's target (LA remodeling contractors). Requires a dedicated software admin to run.

---

### 1.9 Knowify
- **What it does:** Job management for specialty trade contractors — bidding, contracts, change orders, scheduling, daily logs, invoicing, QuickBooks sync. Strong on AIA-style billing and job costing. Per-user pricing.
- **Price:**
  - Essential: ~$99–$186/mo (base + per-user)
  - Advanced: ~$249–$500/mo (base) + $25–40/user above included seats
  - Enterprise: custom
  - For a 10-person team on Advanced: $475–$780/mo all-in
- **Who it's for:** Electricians, plumbers, HVAC installers, specialty subs — not general remodeling. QuickBooks-first shop.
- **Weakness for Obra:** No AI. No video. No Spanish crew tools. Per-user pricing punishes growth. Complex setup. Narrow focus on specialty trades.

---

### 1.10 Procore
- **What it does:** Enterprise construction management OS — project management, financials, quality/safety, BIM, resource management. Unlimited users. Annual Construction Volume-based pricing.
- **Price:** Not published. Reported: ~$375/mo floor for smallest operations. Most contractors: $10,000–$80,000/year. Large GCs: $50,000–$600,000+/year. Implementation: $10,000–$30,000+.
- **Who it's for:** GCs and CMs doing $10M–$1B+ in annual construction. 3M+ users globally but concentrated in large commercial construction.
- **Weakness for Obra:** Completely out of scope for LA remodeling contractors. Overkill. No video AI. No Spanish crew translation. Requires dedicated IT/PM staff to implement and maintain.

### 1.11 New AI-Native Startups (2025–2026) — CRITICAL FINDS

#### Biddio (biddiopro.com) — Launched February 2026
- **What it does:** Homeowner-facing video bidding marketplace. Homeowner records 30-second video walkthrough → AI extracts project specs and measurements → contractors receive real video scope and submit bids → transparent pricing breakdown (labor/materials/overhead/profit).
- **Who it's for:** Homeowners and the contractors who want to receive pre-scoped leads. Marketplace model.
- **Gap vs Obra:** Biddio is homeowner-to-contractor; Obra is contractor-to-crew. Biddio solves lead generation for contractors. Obra solves crew execution and payment. Different workflow, potential partnership angle. BUT: Biddio proves the market validates video-first scoping in construction.

#### Lucho (getlucho.com) — Early Access May 2026
- **What it does:** Bilingual (English/Spanish) crew communication app. Real-time translation preserving trade jargon. Voice/video calls, crew chat, customer texting, auto-tags to jobs. AI summaries and daily briefs.
- **Price:** Not published (early access). Plans: Launch, Hustle, Pro tiers.
- **Who it's for:** Mixed-language construction crews.
- **Gap vs Obra:** Lucho = crew communication only. No estimating, no video scope analysis, no payment. Obra's Video Brain covers the translation angle AND ties it to the work plan and payment. But Lucho validates that Spanish-English translation for crews is a real buying trigger — and they're building a standalone product around it.

#### Workhand (workhand.app)
- **What it does:** Construction software with one-tap chat translation (English/Spanish/Portuguese), AI line-item suggestions from plain-text job description, multilingual estimates and invoices (send docs in customer's language). Field-first, phone-native.
- **Price:** Pro and Team tiers (not published prominently — contact for details).
- **Who it's for:** GCs, remodelers, HVAC, landscapers, pool builders. Built by a contractor turned developer.
- **Gap vs Obra:** Workhand has translation and multilingual docs — the closest feature overlap on the language side. BUT: no video walkthrough analysis, no automatic scope identification from video, no crew work instructions generated from video. Translation is chat-level, not scope-generation level.

#### TradePilot (gettradepilotai.com) — Launching Summer 2026
- **What it does:** AI estimating for handymen and remodelers with built-in LiDAR room scanning (iPhone Pro). FieldScan captures room dimensions in 30 seconds → feeds Pilot AI for estimates using YOUR actual rates, not national averages. Rate calculator for overhead/labor.
- **Price:** Founding pricing: Starter $29/mo, Pro $59/mo (first 100 members).
- **Who it's for:** Handymen and remodelers. Solo to small crew.
- **Gap vs Obra:** LiDAR scanning is clever but requires iPhone Pro and deliberate setup — not a passive video walkthrough. No Spanish/crew translation. No payment flow. Estimates only. Low price signals low ambition.

#### Quotr.ai
- **What it does:** AI takeoff + full estimate builder + proposal generation in one workflow. Positioned as Togal.ai alternative with more complete workflow.
- **Price:** Starting ~$299.90/mo.
- **Who it's for:** Estimators and GCs needing full bid workflow from plans.
- **Gap vs Obra:** Plans-based (uploaded drawings), not field video. No Spanish. No crew instructions.

#### Obra (obra.chat) — Current State
- **What it does:** WhatsApp-based AI back office. Voice/text → quote in 60 seconds. English/Spanish bilingual. Virtual receptionist, expense tracking, Google Sheets sync, lead follow-up. Currently $9/mo after free trial.
- **Note:** This appears to be an EXISTING Obra product at $9/month that is WhatsApp-only. The "Obra" described in this brief (Video Brain, trilingual UI, $5K/mo high tier) is either a rebrand/expansion or a different product entirely. **Critical distinction: the current obra.chat product is a $9/mo WhatsApp tool, not a $5K/mo platform.** The Video Brain described in the brief does not appear to be live yet — this is the product to build toward.

## 3. Top 7 Concrete Moves to Win

### Move 1: Lead With "Your Crew Actually Understands the Job"
**The wedge message:** Every LA remodeling contractor with Mexican crews has lost money because a tile guy laid the wrong pattern or a painter missed a wall — because the scope got lost in translation. No software addresses this. Obra's Video Brain solves it.

**Action:** Cold email subject line: "Do your Mexican crews always understand the plan?" Body: Obra translates your job-site walkthrough video into a step-by-step work plan in Spanish — automatically. No app for the crew. WhatsApp-ready.

**Why competitors can't counter fast:** Workhand has chat translation; Handoff has voice-to-estimate. Neither generates CREW-FACING work instructions in Spanish from a video walkthrough. Building this requires multimodal AI (video understanding + structured output + translation) — 6-12 months minimum to copy.

---

### Move 2: Anchor Pricing Against ServiceTitan's Per-Technician Absurdity
**The play:** ServiceTitan charges $245–$398/technician/month. A 5-tech remodeling crew = $1,225–$1,990/month. And it doesn't even have AI estimating or Spanish tools.

**Obra's Starter at $299/mo** (positioned against what you'd pay ONE technician's software cost on ServiceTitan) looks like a steal. 

**Action:** Build a comparison calculator on the website: "You're running 3 guys. Here's what you'd pay ServiceTitan vs Obra. Here's what Obra does that ServiceTitan literally cannot."

---

### Move 3: Video Brain as the Trojan Horse, Payments as the Profit Engine
**The play:** Lead acquisition through the Video Brain (novel, demonstrable in 60 seconds). Revenue expansion through payment processing (2.59%+ on every invoice). Contractors who use Obra for invoicing become permanent subscribers because switching costs are high (payment history, customer records, price memory).

**Action:** Offer Video Brain free for 30 days with no credit card. Capture payment processing volume at Month 2. Keep the Video Brain locked behind paid tiers ($299+ monthly) after trial.

---

### Move 4: Build "Obra Memory" — The Price-Learning Engine
**The killer feature:** Every time a contractor does a job through Obra, it logs the actual price, scope, and outcome. After 10 jobs, Obra knows this contractor charges $8/sq ft for tile installation in Venice Beach. After 50 jobs, it knows seasonal pricing fluctuations, upsell hit rates, and which job types have highest margin.

**Why this wins:** No competitor has a contractor-specific price memory. Handoff uses generic ZIP-code pricing from Home Depot/Lowe's. Obra learns YOUR prices, YOUR margins, YOUR crew's speed. This compounds — a contractor who has 100 jobs in Obra has a price book that took 2 years to build. They will not leave.

**Action:** Build this into Obra's core data model from day one. Call it "Obra Brain" in marketing.

---

### Move 5: Target the "Night Before the Job" Moment
**The insight:** Remodeling contractors make or lose money in the 30 minutes before a crew starts a job. That's when scope gets explained, misunderstandings happen, and change-order opportunities are missed.

**Action:** Position Obra Video Brain specifically for this moment: "The night before the job, walk your phone through the site. By morning, your crew has their plan in Spanish, your client has an updated scope, and you've already flagged the upsell." This is a ritual, not a feature.

**Marketing:** Short-form video of a contractor doing a 2-minute walkthrough at 8pm → crew arriving next morning with printed Spanish work plan. Post on TikTok/IG targeting LA contractors (Spanish and English).

---

### Move 6: The Upsell Engine — Turn Every Job Into 1.3 Jobs
**The angle:** Obra's Video Brain watches the walkthrough and says: "I see the bathroom needs new tile. I also noticed the grout on the adjacent shower is failing and the vanity caulk is cracked. Add these as optional upsells to the estimate?"

**Why this justifies $1,000–$5,000/month pricing:** If a contractor does $50K/month in jobs and Obra's upsell suggestions convert at 20% add-on rate at 15% margin, that's $1,500/month in incremental profit. Obra pays for itself at $299/month. At $5,000/month, the value must be demonstrated through documented upsell revenue — build this tracking into the dashboard.

**Action:** Track and display "Obra-suggested upsells accepted" and "Revenue added through Obra" prominently in the contractor dashboard.

---

### Move 7: Own the LA Spanish-Speaking Contractor Market With WhatsApp-Native Distribution
**The insight:** The Apify + GMass cold email approach reaches English-reading contractor owners. But the real decision-maker for crew efficiency is often a bilingual foreman or the owner themselves — both of whom live on WhatsApp.

**Action:** 
- WhatsApp broadcast list for LA Spanish-speaking contractors (build via Facebook Groups, Spanish-language contractor forums, Instagram)
- Obra works IN WhatsApp (voice notes, estimates, crew instructions) — this is a distribution moat. Competitors require downloading an app. Obra's adoption friction is near zero for Spanish-speaking contractors.
- Referral program: "Get one contractor to pay, get one month free" — viral within the community.

## 4. The Single Sharpest Wedge

**"Video Brain + Spanish Crew Instructions"** — verified uncontested as of June 2026.

No competitor has a product that:
- Accepts a job-site walkthrough VIDEO as input (not photos, not PDFs, not voice notes)
- Uses AI to IDENTIFY what work needs to be done (not just transcribe what the contractor says)
- GENERATES step-by-step crew instructions in Spanish
- Ties those instructions to a price estimate and payment flow

Why competitors can't copy this fast:
- Togal.ai and Quotr.ai are plan-document companies — video is a different modality
- Handoff.ai is voice/photo-first — video analysis requires a different AI stack and UX
- Workhand has translation but not AI scene understanding from video
- Lucho has bilingual comms but no estimating
- Biddio has video scope but is homeowner-facing

The Spanish-crew instruction layer is specifically hard to replicate because it requires: multimodal video AI + construction domain knowledge + bilingual structured output + crew-psychology understanding (crew instructions ≠ client proposals). That's 4 separate hard problems solved together.

**Competitors who could theoretically copy this:** Handoff.ai (closest). But their "AI site walkthroughs" feature is photo-based notes, not video-to-structured-crew-plan. ETA to copy: 12–18 months minimum, assuming they prioritize it.

**Key insight:** Contractors paying $300–$800/mo is the norm for established SMBs. $1,000–$2,000/mo is achievable for companies with 5–15 employees. $5,000/mo requires demonstrable ROI (revenue added or costs saved), not just features.

**Tier 1 — Starter: $149/mo**
- Video Brain: up to 10 videos/mo
- Spanish work plans
- Estimate + invoice generation
- WhatsApp delivery
- 1 user

**Tier 2 — Pro: $399/mo**
- Unlimited Video Brain
- Price memory (Obra Brain learns your rates)
- Upsell engine
- 5 users + crew WhatsApp delivery
- Basic payment processing (2.5%)
- Competes with: Handoff Pro ($299/mo) + Workhand — wins on video AI and payment integration

**Tier 3 — Business: $999/mo**
- Everything in Pro

- Competes with: Knowify Advanced ($500–$780/mo), Buildertrend Essential ($400–$500/mo) — wins on AI + Spanish + lower price

**Tier 4 — Scale: $2,500/mo**
- Everything in Business

**Tier 5 — Enterprise: $5,000/mo**
- Custom implementation
- Multi-location management

### Can $5,000/Month Be Justified?

**Yes — but only with documented ROI.** The path:
1. Starter and Pro tiers prove the tool works (upsells accepted, payments faster, crew errors down)
2. Obra dashboard tracks "Revenue added through Obra" and "Rework incidents avoided"
3. At Scale/Enterprise tiers, Obra sells ROI, not features: "Our Scale customers average $28K/month in Obra-attributed revenue"
4. Annual contracts at Scale and Enterprise reduce churn and justify higher support investment

**The number that unlocks $5K/mo pricing:** A contractor grossing $200K/month where Obra adds 5% in upsell revenue ($10K) and saves 2% in rework cost ($4K) = $14K/mo value. $5K/mo is 36% of value delivered — a reasonable SaaS take rate.
