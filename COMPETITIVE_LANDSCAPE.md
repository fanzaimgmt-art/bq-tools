# Obra — Competitive Landscape (20/6/2026)

Mapped from live sites + the Chuck the Contractor channel (customer voice).

## The 3 layers of the space
1. **Takeoff / estimating (heavy AI/CV, big players)**
   - **Togal.ai** — AI takeoff, measures quantities from drawings, "98% accuracy," "5x faster," "Takeoff in Minutes. Not Days." Target: estimators + big GCs (DPR, Choate, Consigli). Demo-led, premium. Heavy named social proof.
   - **Perplexity Computer** — blueprint → bid-ready estimate (agentic: extract scope, research live material/labor costs, phase breakdown, export to Sheets/Excel/Notion, remembers vendor pricing, material-cost alerts). Big player entering.
2. **All-in-one project management (established, broad, $$$)**
   - **Buildertrend** — "#1 construction mgmt," 20,000+ builders, complex high-value projects, scheduling + financials + client comms + embedded AI. Enterprise-ish, premium, demo-led. 4.5 Capterra / 4.3 G2.
   - **Contractor Foreman** — all-in-one (project mgmt, financials, people), **$49/mo**, "easy + affordable," small/medium contractors, 4.5 stars, 100-day guarantee, demo-led. Loved for design + ease.
3. **Light estimate → invoice → get-paid (Obra's lane)**
   - **OnSite (contractorai.work)** — ⚠️ **DIRECT competitor.** AI estimate in 60s → email estimate → customer approval → payments (card/ACH) → automated reminders ("never chase payments") → profit tracking + receipt scanning. Mobile app (iOS). "137+ contractors," "win 40% more bids," ~$20/mo, no contracts. This is ~80% of Obra's current money MVP, already shipping.
   - **Emergent** — generic AI website builder pointed at construction (portfolio, quote requests, bid workflows). Not really the money wedge — a Lovable/Bolt clone with a construction landing page.

## What this means for Obra (the honest read)
- **Demand is validated** (OnSite 137, Contractor Foreman, Buildertrend 20K) — but the "estimate + invoice + get-paid" tool ALREADY EXISTS (OnSite), cheaper, mobile, with users. Obra cannot win by being "another one of those."
- **Don't go all-in-one** (Buildertrend/Contractor Foreman own that, and it's the 18-tool-sprawl trap again). Stay sharp.
- **Pricing reality:** self-serve ~$20–$49/mo (OnSite $20, CF $49); the differentiated money is the **Done-For-You $500–$5K concierge** nobody else offers.

## ✅ Obra's WIDE-OPEN wedge (nobody owns these)
1. **The Video Brain** (see `OBRA_VISION_VIDEO_BRAIN.md`) — phone job-site video → AI understands it → **multilingual crew instructions (Spanish!)** + work plan + price + upsell suggestions. NONE of the 6 do this. This is the hero differentiator.
2. **Spanish-native for the crew** — every competitor is English-corporate. Obra (the name is Spanish for "the work") speaking to the **Mexican/Latino workforce** is a real, ownable angle. The crew, not just the boss, is a user.
3. **GC-compliance doc layer** (W9/COI auto-attach) + **zero-login client pay page** + **Done-For-You tier** — OnSite has payments but not these.

## Strategic recommendation
Reframe Obra's hero from generic "estimate + get paid" (OnSite's lane) to:
> **"The AI foreman: point your phone at the job — Obra explains it to your crew in their language, prices it, and finds your upsells. Then gets you paid."**
Lead with the **Video Brain + bilingual crew** as the differentiator; keep get-paid (invoice/tracker/docs) as the proven backbone. Target the small/solo contractor with a Latino crew — the segment the big players ignore.
