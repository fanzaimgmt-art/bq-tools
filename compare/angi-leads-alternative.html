When Angi still makes sense, honestly

If you're a brand-new business with zero pipeline and need any leads in the door this week while you build your own marketing — Angi can be a legitimate (expensive) bridge. Read the contract terms before you sign, and know what the early-cancellation penalty is.

But every lead you buy is a customer someone else can also buy. Once you have any pipeline at all, the move is to pair your own marketing (Google Local Services Ads, your own site, referrals) with a system that keeps every quote, invoice and client relationship you build — permanently yours.
