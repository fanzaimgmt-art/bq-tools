# Obra — "Video Brain" feature spec (Moshe's idea, 20/6/2026)

The differentiator no competitor (OnSite, Togal, Perplexity Computer) has. Independently validated: Chuck the Contractor describes the exact "walkthrough video → AI second brain" workflow as the killer contractor AI use case; Moshe arrived at the same thing from the field.

## The real pain (Moshe, from the field)
Contractors film a video of the plan/work (e.g. "demolition here, this wall, this much") and drop it in a WhatsApp group. It's often NOT explained well — and the **Mexican crew doesn't always understand** what to do. Result: mistakes, redo, delays.

## The feature: point your phone at the job → Obra does the rest
A "super computer" inside Obra that takes a job-site video and:
1. **Understands every detail** in the video — run MULTIPLE models at once (Gemini + Claude + Grok), like Moshe's `/watch` skill, built into the system. Frames + transcript + reasoning.
2. **Translates to any language** — so the Mexican crew (or any worker) gets clear instructions in THEIR language. (Spanish first — huge for US construction labor.)
3. **Generates a real work plan** — from-where-to-where, what, why, how much. Step-by-step the crew can follow.
4. **Generates reference images** — roughly what the result should look like / what to do (nano-banana / image-gen).
5. **Suggests upsells** — spots things worth doing that can raise the price (the move good salespeople make walking a house). "How to increase the ticket via the video."
6. **Learns the business's prices** → becomes "the brain of the business" → knows how to quote that contractor's work.
7. **Bundles a Construction skill** — pricebooks, how to quote, how long things take, processes — all in one place.

## Why it's the wedge
- OnSite = type a job → estimate. Togal = blueprint → takeoff. Perplexity = blueprint → estimate. **None take a phone VIDEO → multilingual crew instructions + plan + upsell.** That's the gap.
- It fuses Moshe's real edges: the `/watch` multi-model skill, AI video/image gen (Kolbo/Seedance/nano-banana), and the bilingual (EN/ES/HE) angle.
- It reframes Obra from "get paid faster" to **"point your phone at the job — Obra explains it to your crew in their language, prices it, and finds your upsells."**

## MVP cut (don't boil the ocean — ship after the money MVP is solid)
Phase 1 (smallest useful): upload job video → transcript + frame analysis (one model first, e.g. Gemini for video) → a clean bilingual (EN+ES) step list the contractor can send the crew. 
Phase 2: + price estimate learned from the contractor's pricebook + upsell suggestions.
Phase 3: + reference image generation + multi-model consensus.
